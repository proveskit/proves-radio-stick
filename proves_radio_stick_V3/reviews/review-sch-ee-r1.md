# Radio Stick V3 schematic review, round 1 (principal EE)

Goal restated: get the STM32U575VIT6 radio stick order-ready at JLCPCB tonight, with an MCU footprint that also takes the STM32U5A5VJT6 (OTG_HS PHY on PA11/PA12) later.

Evidence sources used. Netlist: `$WORK/v3.net.xml`, dumped with `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/674b3aef-f740-5b2a-bc1d-98dbdf2f1ed2/scratchpad/rev_ee/nets.py` to `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/674b3aef-f740-5b2a-bc1d-98dbdf2f1ed2/scratchpad/rev_ee/nets.txt`. Pin data: ST open pin data XML for STM32U575VITx and STM32U5A5VJTx (power pins and signals diffed by script). ERC: `$WORK/erc.rpt` (KiCad 10, 06:31 today). Web: AN5373 "Power supply management" chapter (st.com HTML chunk); LCSC product pages for C13738, C97604, C1547, C23733, C32949, C5270999, C22438122; Ebyte E22-900M30S manual (sister part of the E22-400M30S, same M30S family; the 400 MHz manual itself was not fetched); ST community thread "STM32U5A5 PB5 and PB15 failing" (ST reply on UCPD dead-battery pull-downs). Anything else is labelled "memory".

Blockers: none found. Majors: 3.

| EE1-01 | major | Radio iface / MCU pin choice | Radio NSS sits on PA15 = UCPD1_CC1, and RXEN sits on PB5 = UCPD1_DBCC1. On U5 the dead-battery Rd (~5.1k) on CCx is switched in by a high level on DBCCx until firmware sets PWR_UCPDR.UCPD_DBDIS. So the first time firmware drives RXEN high (PB5 high) before DBDIS is set, NSS gets a 5.1k pull-down against R9 10k pull-up: idle NSS ~ 3.3*5.1/15.1 = 1.1 V, an invalid level, and SPI to the SX1268 fails. Same parts, same pins on U5A5. A user on U5A5 hit exactly this on PA15/PB5; ST's answer was that these pins carry UCPD dead-battery pull-downs; disabling dead battery in firmware did not fully fix that user. | opd: pin 77 PA15 has `UCPD1_CC1`, pin 91 PB5 has `UCPD1_DBCC1` (both parts). Netlist: `SPI1_CS0` = R9.1, U1.77, U3.19; `RF1_RX_EN` = R11.2, U1.91, U3.6. ST community thread quote: "The pull-down on PB15 (UCPD1_CC2) is activated by high level on PB14 (UCPD1_DBCC2)", same for PA15/PB5. | Move RF1_RX_EN from PB5 (pin 91) to PC7 (pin 64, unused, no UCPD/JTAG function per opd). Leave PB5 unconnected (analog after reset, DBCC1 stays low). Keep NSS on PA15 with R9 10k to +3V3. Firmware: set PWR_UCPDR.UCPD_DBDIS=1 in early init anyway. Update pinmap.csv and the Zephyr overlay `rx-enable-gpios`. |
| EE1-02 | major | J3 breakout | J3_IO6 = PB14 (UCPD1_DBCC2) and J3_IO7 = PB15 (UCPD1_CC2). Anything external that pulls J3 pin 7 high turns on a 5.1k pull-down on J3 pin 8 (SPI2_MOSI) until DBDIS is set, and the CC2 analog front end sits on a screw-terminal pin. Not a damage risk, but a field-confusing SPI2 failure and a footprint-transfer hazard to the U5A5 flight board. | opd: pin 53 PB14 `SPI2_MISO, UCPD1_DBCC2`; pin 54 PB15 `SPI2_MOSI, UCPD1_CC2`; pin 17 PC2 `SPI2_MISO`; pin 18 PC3 `SPI2_MOSI`. Netlist: J3_IO6 = J3.7, U1.53; J3_IO7 = J3.8, U1.54. | Move J3_IO6 (SPI2_MISO) to PC2 (pin 17) and J3_IO7 (SPI2_MOSI) to PC3 (pin 18), both unused today. Keep SPI2_SCK on PB13. Leave PB14/PB15 unconnected. No BOM change. |
| EE1-03 | major (inherited) | 5 V / radio power | Worst-case VBUS load is ~650 mA radio TX (E22 M30S, 30 dBm) + ~30-40 mA for the 3V3 buck input, ~0.7 A total. A USB 2.0 port or a USB-C source advertising Default power only guarantees 500 mA. Nothing in hardware limits this; the CC sense to ADC exists for that decision but no rule is written down. | Ebyte E22-900M30S manual sec. 2.2: TX current 650 mA typ, VCC 2.5-5.5 V, ">=5.0V ensures output power". Netlist: `VBUS` feeds FB1 -> U19 (TPS22918) -> RF_VCC (U3.9/10) and U2 (TPS62085) VIN. CC thresholds (memory, USB Type-C spec vRd): Default 0.25-0.61 V, 1.5 A 0.70-1.16 V, 3.0 A 1.31-2.04 V. | No hardware change. Write the firmware rule into PLAN.md: read PB1/PB2 at boot; allow 30 dBm only when max(CC1,CC2) > 0.66 V (1.5 A or 3 A advertised); else cap TX at about 22 dBm or refuse TX. Print the label "needs 1.5 A USB-C" on the silk. |
| EE1-04 | minor | Clocks, LSE | LSE crystal is Seiko SC-32S 7 pF (C97604). 10 pF caps give CL ~ 5 + 2.5 (stray) = 7.5 pF, which matches. But the critical gm is ~0.76 uA/V (ESR 70 kOhm, C0 1 pF, both memory), above the U5 LSE "low drive" Gmcritmax (memory, ~0.5 uA/V). | LCSC C97604 page: "SC-32S32.768kHz20PPM7pF". C117/C118 = C32949 10 pF C0G (LCSC page). gm_crit = 4*ESR*(2*pi*f)^2*(C0+CL)^2 per AN2867 (formula from memory). | Keep parts. Firmware: set LSEDRV to medium-high (Zephyr `&clk_lse { driving-capability = <2>; }`). Put this in the Zephyr board file. |
| EE1-05 | note | Clocks, HSE | HSE Y101 X322516MLB4SI is CL 9 pF, ESR 50 Ohm (C13738). 12 pF C0G caps give 6 + ~3 pF stray = 9 pF, correct. gm_crit ~ 4*50*(2*pi*16e6)^2*(3p+9p)^2 = 0.29 mA/V (C0=3 pF assumed); U5 HSE Gmcritmax ~1.5 mA/V (memory), ratio ~5. 16 MHz is a valid input for PLL1 -> 48 MHz USB (no CRS in Zephyr) and is one of the OTG_HS PHY reference frequencies on U5A5 (memory: 16/19.2/20/24/26/32 MHz, SYSCFG_OTGHSPHYCR.CLKSEL). | LCSC C13738: CL 9 pF, ESR 50 Ohm, +/-10 ppm. C115/C116 = C1547 0402CG120J500NT 12 pF C0G. Netlist: Y101.1 = OSC_IN/PH0, Y101.3 = OSC_OUT/PH1, Y101.2/.4 = GND. | None. Layout: crystal within 5 mm of pins 12/13, guard ground, no signals under it. |
| EE1-06 | minor (inherited) | Radio power | Radio rail defaults ON. R95 10k pulls RF_PWR_EN (PB4) to +3V3, and PB4 is NJTRST, which has an internal pull-up from reset (memory). So at USB attach the TPS22918 charges C4 100 uF + C5 10 uF + C6 at once. Default-on also makes the radio power-cycle path depend on firmware. A pull-down alone cannot fix it: a 10k pull-down against the ~40k NJTRST pull-up sits at ~0.66 V, between TPS22918 VIL 0.5 V and VIH 1.0 V (memory). | Netlist: `RF_PWR_EN` = R95.1, U1.90 (PB4), U19.3 (ON); R95.2 on +3V3. opd: pin 90 "PB4 (NJTRST)". | Recommended: move RF_PWR_EN to a pin with no reset pull, e.g. PD8 (pin 55, unused), and make R95 10k to GND (C25744, same part). Radio then stays off until firmware enables it. If PB4 is kept, change R95 to 4.7k to GND (C25900) so the divider is <=0.3 V. |
| EE1-07 | minor (inherited) | Radio iface | Back-powering. With RF_VCC switched off, R9 (NSS), R5 (NRST), R14 (BUSY), R15 (DIO1) still pull radio pins to +3V3, so the unpowered module is fed through its IO clamp diodes. That defeats RF_PWR_EN as a clean radio power cycle. BUSY and DIO1 are SX126x push-pull outputs and need no pull-up. | Netlist: R9.2/R5.2/R14.1/R15.1 on +3V3; RF1_IO0 = R14.2, U3.14 (BUSY); RF1_IO1 = R15.2, U3.13 (DIO1). | DNP R14 and R15 (or delete them). Firmware: before turning RF_PWR_EN off, drive NSS, NRST, SCK, MOSI, TXEN, RXEN low. Keep R9 for NSS idle during reset. |
| EE1-08 | minor | J3 breakout | J3 IO pins go straight to MCU pins with no series resistance or ESD on a screw terminal. PA4 (J3_IO8) is a DAC/ADC pin; on U5 it is TT_a, not 5 V tolerant (memory, DS13737 pin table not fetched). FT pins also only tolerate VDD+4 V, so 5 V into an unpowered stick is outside the abs max (memory). | Netlist: J3_IO1..8 = U1.23, 24, 15, 16, 52, 53, 54, 29; J3.1 and J3.10 GND only. opd: pin 29 PA4 `DAC1_OUT1`. | Add 100 Ohm 0402 series resistors on J3_IO1..8 (C25076, Basic) at the MCU side. Swap J3_IO8 from PA4 to an FT pin, e.g. PC8 (pin 65, unused) if DAC is not needed. Silk: "3V3 logic only". |
